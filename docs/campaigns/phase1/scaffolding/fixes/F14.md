## Constats à corriger

### A1 [major] service/engine.py:601 — A death before t0 is never judged, and the match runs on with a dead participant

**Preuve de la revue** : Spec section Fin, 'Mort subite | player_death d'un camp | death, l'autre camp gagne immédiatement', has no exception for the countdown. Before running, _take_event (engine.py:1045) only journals the event, because _core is None. _enter_running (engine.py:601) builds a fresh MatchCore without looking at earlier deaths, and rules.py:881 requires t_ms >= t0. The mod does not report it again: reportDeath latches deadPlayers[name] (DuelCore.lua:501) and checkDeaths clears the latch only once the player is seen alive. Probe run through the real Engine and doubles: player_death emitted during countdown gave phase=running, result=None at te=0 and still at te=5. The window is wider than the 3 s countdown, because README step 2 tells both players to get wounded in the lobby just before t0. This repeats X14 from the earlier review. No text under docs/campaigns/phase1/scaffolding/fixes, no test and no entry in limites-connues.md covers it. Not verified: what a dead participant does in the real game (stays online, respawns or disconnects). Fix: at _enter_running, refuse or adjudicate a participant with a journaled death, and add a countdown-death test.

**Correction attendue** : Une mort pendant le décompte doit être jugée, pas ignorée : au passage en `running`, le moteur examine les `player_death` déjà journalisés avant `t0`. Soit il adjuge immédiatement (l'autre camp gagne, conformément à la règle de mort subite), soit il refuse le lancement avec un motif explicite — jamais un match qui démarre avec un participant déjà mort. Test : mort émise pendant le décompte → au premier tick de match, résultat décidé (ou refus nommé), jamais `result=None` à `te=5`.

### A2 [major] service/engine.py:290 — The hook proof is demanded per match, so ordinary default-preset games stall 120 s and lose an advertised rule

**Preuve de la revue** : ContractWindow.due (engine.py:290-296) holds the lobby until OnPlayerGetDamage is proven on both sides or L + hook_window_s passes, as spec line 230 and AC67 require. The mod emits that proof only on a real bleeding wound (DuelCore.lua:457-476). So unless both players deliberately get wounded before the match, every duel_rapide or pression launch waits the full 120 s, then withdraws damage_taken. That removes hurt_supply, and in pression also untouched_minute and the combo, through narrow_table (rules.py:237). Meanwhile the UI summary still advertises '1 blessure reçue → caisse de soin', and the sealed rules_hash still names the full table. README:59 confirms the wound is the only way to keep the atom. The proof answers a question about the game build and mod (does this hook fire on this server?), not about this match, yet players pay for it in every match with a pre-match injury or a lost rule. limites-connues.md:13 also admits the hook may never fire on the server, in which case the atom is withdrawn from every match. This conforms to the spec, so the finding is against the design. Fix: prove the hook once per build and mod fingerprint and cache the result, or derive damage from a server-side health delta in step(), and show the played table in the lobby.

**Correction attendue** : La preuve du hook ne doit plus se payer à chaque match. Établis-la une fois par couple (build du jeu, empreinte du mod) et réutilise-la : le lobby ne patiente plus 120 s, et le preset par défaut ne perd plus ses règles faute de blessure préalable. La détection des dégâts doit reposer sur un signal serveur (delta de points de vie dans `step()`) avec le hook comme confirmation, et la table jouée doit être celle annoncée dans l'UI et scellée dans `rules_hash`. Tests : deuxième match sans attente ; aucun atome retiré sans preuve d'échec réelle ; `hurt_supply` actif sans blessure pré-match.

### A3 [minor] mods/duelcore/42.21/media/lua/server/DuelCore.lua:977 — The mod's 1 s write buffer has no settling barrier, so conclusions can ignore earlier facts

**Preuve de la revue** : emit buffers lines and flushOut runs once per FLUSH_MS = 1000 (DuelCore.lua:44, 977-981), with unsynchronised phases on A and B. Service-side care covers only what is already in the file: _aim_past_conclusion (engine.py:623) re-reads to the end of out, then _judged_tick concludes at once. tests/test_match.py:1953 states the intended rule, 'La mort la plus ancienne décide', but only with both lines already written. Probe with the real Engine, simulating the buffer: A's death dated 400 ms after B's, with A's line flushed first, gave outcome=death, winner=B, so the player who died first won. Second probe: a kill dated before the timer but flushed after it was dropped, and the result stayed a 0-0 draw. A kill in the last second can therefore flip a one-point match, depending only on flush phase. Fix: hold any conclusion for one flush interval plus a margin, then re-read both out files, or have the mod flush immediately on player_death.

**Correction attendue** : La conclusion ne doit plus courir après le tampon d'écriture du mod (1 s, phases non synchronisées entre A et B). Deux verrous : le mod vide son tampon immédiatement sur `player_death` (et sur tout fait qui peut conclure), et le service, avant de conclure, attend un intervalle de vidage plus une marge puis relit les deux fichiers. Tests : mort la plus ancienne écrite en second → c'est bien elle qui décide ; kill daté avant l'échéance mais vidé après → il compte.
