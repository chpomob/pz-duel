## Constats à corriger

### A4 [major] service/effects.json:4 — The damage_taken hook proof does not prove the atom can fire

**Preuve de la revue** : ContractWindow treats hook_seen(OnWeaponHitCharacter) as proof for damage_taken (effects.json:4, engine.py:257-260). DuelCore.lua:468 emits hook_seen before the handler runs, and lines 372-373 discard every hit whose target is not a player. The README procedure satisfies the proof by a player hitting a zombie, which exercises only the discarded branch. Assumption: if zombie attacks on players do not fire this hook on the server, the contract still passes and the atom never fires. hurt_supply then never triggers, the pression combo never resets, and 'not atom(damage_taken)' in untouched_minute is always true, so scoring pressure changes silently during a 'valid' match. The README concedes the gap, and only a manual checklist after the match catches it. Confirm by observing a zombie bite in play; better, make the proof the first damage_taken event or a dedicated probe.

**Correction attendue** : hook_seen doit être émis à la première réception RÉELLE du hook, pas à son enregistrement. Pour damage_taken, le mod ne doit émettre hook_seen qu'au premier OnWeaponHitCharacter dont la cible est le joueur du serveur ; caps continue de publier l'enregistrement, distinct de la preuve. Ajoute les tests de la distinction (enregistré sans preuve ≠ vu).

### B5 [major] service/rules.py:834 — Check command-result ownership before releasing an effect

**Preuve de la revue** : Settlement receives only data.seq and never checks event.src against the command's target. A protocol-valid cmd_result from A naming an outstanding command sent to B releases B's concurrency slot and removes its unanswered tracking, although B has not completed the command. Pass the source into settlement and reject results whose source does not match the tracked target.

**Correction attendue** : La libération d'une place d'effet doit se faire sur la commande identifiée par son seq : un cmd_result qui ne correspond à aucune commande en cours (ou à une commande déjà libérée) est journalisé et ne libère rien. Teste un résultat orphelin et un double résultat.

### A13 [nit] mods/duelcore/42.21/media/lua/server/DuelCore.lua:352 — lastAttacker keeps strong references to zombies that are hit but never die

**Preuve de la revue** : onHitZombie stores lastAttacker[zombie] (line 352), and only onZombieDead clears it (line 362). The table is not weak, so zombies that are hit and then despawn stay referenced for the server's lifetime. The line 364 comment says the game recycles zombies, so a stale entry could also be read for a recycled zombie. Growth is small over a 25-minute match with one player, and the side result is unchanged because a server hosts one side.

**Correction attendue** : lastAttacker ne doit pas retenir indéfiniment des zombies touchés mais jamais tués : purge l'entrée à la mort du zombie et borne la table (ou purge périodique). Test statique ou unitaire du comportement de purge.

### B3 [major] mods/duelcore/42.21/media/lua/server/DuelCore.lua:261 — Reject infinite sequence numbers before advancing the command floor

**Preuve de la revue** : decodeNumber accepts an overflowing exponent such as 1e999 as infinity, and math.floor(value)==value also holds for infinity. acceptCommandLine therefore accepts this seq and sets lastCmdSeq to infinity. Every subsequent finite command is discarded, while encodeNumber serializes the acknowledgement sequence as null. Require finite, positive command sequence numbers before changing lastCmdSeq.

**Correction attendue** : Le Lua doit rejeter un seq non fini ou hors bornes avant de faire avancer cmd_floor et lastCmdSeq, et journaliser le refus, sans interrompre la boucle.
